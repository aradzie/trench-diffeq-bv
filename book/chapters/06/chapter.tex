\chapter{Applications of Linear Second Order Equations}

\noindent
IN THIS CHAPTER we study applications of linear second order equations.

\medskip
\noindent
SECTIONS~6.1 AND 6.2 are about spring--mass systems.

\medskip
\noindent
SECTION~6.3 is about $RLC$ circuits, the electrical analogs of spring--mass systems.

\medskip
\noindent
SECTION~6.4 is about motion of an object under a central force, which is particularly relevant in
the space age, since, for example, a satellite moving in orbit subject only to Earth's gravity is
experiencing motion under a central force.
